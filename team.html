Everyone who works in your agency’s workspace and what they can do

Roles. Sales creates clients, sets appointments, edits customer info and hands clients to a consultant; sees every analysis, price and ROI but cannot change them. Consultant runs and edits analyses and pricing and manages clients. Admin does everything a consultant does, plus this page.
Adding someone. Give them the agency invite code (the AGENCY_INVITE_CODE value in Vercel). They create their own account at the sign-up page and start as Sales. Come back here to change their role. Deactivating someone locks them out immediately and keeps their history.
